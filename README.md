# Hakushin Ring

A display prop of the Hakushin Ring: a domed deep-purple ring with a lilac
inner band, a gold horned crest held by a white collar, gold clasps and beads,
a medallion at the top and a knotted tassel on each side. It stands in a
cradle on a walnut base and lifts out to be held. 178 mm wide, 211 mm tall on
its stand, about 375 g.

The whole design lives in `hakushin-ring.json`: its parameters, parts,
assembly, connections and eleven requirements. `hakushin-ring.step` is the
same assembly as solid geometry, for a slicer or any CAD tool.

The Hakushin Ring is a design from Genshin Impact by HoYoverse. This is a fan
build for personal use.

The ring is the largest print and needs a 180 mm bed. 0.16 mm layers on the
ring, band and crest keep the domes smooth; sand and clear-coat them if you
want a polished finish.

## After printing

- Drill the eye in each tassel hanger to 2.2 mm.
- Drill the base 4.5 mm through the centre and counterbore it 8 x 5 from below.
- Fill the three dimples in the medallion with white paint.

## Assembly

1. Glue the inner band into the step on the back of the ring. The two backs
   finish flush.
2. Epoxy the collar under the crest, then under the bottom of the band, with
   everything face down on a flat surface so the backs line up. Each joint is
   a 4 mm step.
3. Glue the two clasps over the ring 40 degrees above horizontal on each side,
   and the two tassel hangers 20 degrees below horizontal, eyes pointing out.
4. Glue a brass bead into each dimple near the top of the ring.
5. Glue the petals over the top of the ring between the beads, then the rim
   and the medallion onto the petals.
6. Open each jump ring, pass it through a hanger's eye and close it. Tie the
   cord through it in a two-loop bow, tie the tassel to the tail, and glue a
   cap over the tassel's head.
7. Melt the insert into the bottom of the cradle and screw the cradle to the
   base from below.
8. Stand the ring in the cradle's groove.

## Checks the model makes

At the dimensions in this repository all eleven requirements are met and no
two parts intersect: the ring fits the print bed, every glued step is at least
3 mm wide, the horns clear the band, the gold shells stop short of the band,
the cradle holds 14 mm of the ring, the tassels hang clear of the base and
15 mm or more above the table, and the base is wide enough not to tip.

The model checks geometry, not strength or print tolerances. The clasps,
hangers and petals are modelled as an exact fit on the ring, so sand them
lightly if your printer runs tight.
